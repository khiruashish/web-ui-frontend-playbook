## Experience levels

Every topic carries a badge so you know who it's written for. Beginners can skip the orange and red parts on a first read; seniors can jump straight to them.

| Badge | Level | Experience | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="lvl lvl-1">Beginner</span> | 🟢 | 0–2 years | What it is, why it exists, the simplest working example |
| <span class="lvl lvl-2">Intermediate</span> | 🟡 | 2–5 years | Real-world usage, common mistakes, browser quirks |
| <span class="lvl lvl-3">Advanced</span> | 🟠 | 5–10 years | Accessibility, performance, architecture, edge cases |
| <span class="lvl lvl-4">Expert</span> | 🔴 | 10–15+ years | Design systems, scale, trade-offs, design-review judgement |
