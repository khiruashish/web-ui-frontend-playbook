    Experience levels¶

    Every topic carries a badge so you know who it's written for. Beginners can skip the orange and red parts on a first read; seniors can jump straight to them.

    Badge Level Experience Focus
    Beginner 🟢 0–2 years What it is, why it exists, the simplest working example
    Intermediate 🟡 2–5 years Real-world usage, common mistakes, browser quirks
    Advanced 🟠 5–10 years Accessibility, performance, architecture, edge cases
    Expert 🔴 10–15+ years Design systems, scale, trade-offs, design-review judgement
